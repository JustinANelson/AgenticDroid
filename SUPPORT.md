# Support

AgenticDroid is experimental and has no supported binary release, paid support, or guaranteed
response time. Community help is best effort.

## Where to ask

- Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/JustinANelson/AgenticDroid/discussions) for setup
  problems, usage questions, design conversation, and troubleshooting that may benefit others.
- Use the **Bug report** form for reproducible incorrect behavior.
- Use the **Feature request** form for a concrete improvement proposal.
- Use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

Before opening an issue, search existing issues and read [README.md](README.md),
[DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md), and the relevant troubleshooting section. Include the
commit SHA, Android/device details, execution environment, exact commands, and sanitized logs.

## What maintainers cannot provide

Maintainers cannot debug private repositories, recover lost workspaces or credentials, operate
your SSH/LAN hosts, provide agent-provider account support, or guarantee compatibility with
unlisted devices and downloaded tools. Never share credentials or private source to obtain help.

Questions or issues may be closed when they lack enough information, concern an unsupported binary,
belong to an upstream provider, or cannot be reproduced. A clear minimal reproduction is the best
way to help someone help you.
